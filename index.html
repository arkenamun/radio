Example recordings

You can’t hear your own signal. This is what others hear.

1Hum on the signal

Listen for the steady low buzz under the voice. The words are lost in it.

Cause: a handheld transmitting from its charger, indoors.

Fix: lift it out of the charger and step outside.

2Two stations at once

Listen for the warble. Stations answered a call for “any station?” at the same moment, and none of them can be read.

Fix: listen before you transmit. If you hear this, ask them to say again, one at a time.

Recorded from routine radio scheds and edited so that no caller can be identified. Draft for Training Working Group review, November 2026.
