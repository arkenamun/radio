Tait vehicle radio yellow head · VHF + UHF, and a second VHF where a yellow handset is fitted

Change channel
Key the number on the handset, then Select. Or ▲▼ to scroll, then Done.
Swap VHF / UHF
F3 BAND. The large channel on the display is the radio you talk on.
Hear both radios
F1 DUAL MODE: both channels show, the one you talk on in large type. Press again to transmit on both; hold 3 seconds for one radio.
Talk-Around
F4 TALK AROUND, shown top left of the display. F4 again, or change channel, to cancel.
Scan
F2 SCAN. Keep it off at incidents unless the IC directs.
Your location
Describe where you are. Coordinates only when requested: Menu › GPS, ▲▼ for lat/long or Zone 50 Easting/Northing.

Your location
No GPS. Describe where you are; give coordinates only if requested.

Use it outdoors, away from the vehicle and obstacles. Not indoors, not for air operations or as ground controller. On 173 it may hear the repeater but not reach it.

TALK-AROUND RULE
Use Talk-Around only when the IC directs, or if you declare an emergency and get no reply: then ask to be relayed. Sheet 3 shows how it works in a radio shadow.

Talking on the Radio

Call signs, prowords and the phonetic alphabet.

RADIO USER GUIDE
1WAERN Radios
2Talking on the Radio
3Common Radio Issues

Making a call

1
Listen first. Wait until the channel is clear and you've heard OUT.
2
Press PTT, wait one second, then talk.
3
Call them, then say THIS IS and your call sign.
OVER
My turn is finished. A reply is needed.
OUT
Finished, no reply. Only one station says it.

Call signs

DFES lists when they're needed: your first call, a station joining or leaving, changing channel, closing down, difficult conditions and multi-agency incidents. Otherwise they can be left out, as in the example.

You
"Denmark Chief, this is Shadforth Light Tanker, over."
Denmark Chief
"Shadforth Light Tanker, this is Denmark Chief, go ahead, over."
You
"Arrived on scene, two crew, starting on the north flank, over."
Denmark Chief
"Roger, out."

Moving off the command channel

Keep 173 for command. Crews work on 360, then 361, 362.

1
"CHANGE TO 360, OVER." Get ready.
2
"WILCO, OVER." Or say if you can't.
3
"CHANGE NOW, OUT." Go to 360.
4
Radio check on 360 straight away.

Common prowords

WAIT
Short pause. Others don't transmit.
WAIT OUT
Long pause. Others may transmit.
ROGER
Message understood. Not a "yes".
WILCO
Understood, will comply.
SAY AGAIN
Repeat your last message.
CORRECTION
Error. The right version follows.
GO AHEAD
Send your message.
SITREP
What is your situation?

Emergency message extreme emergency only · everyone else stops transmitting and listens

"EMERGENCY EMERGENCY EMERGENCY, Denmark Chief, this is Firefighter Smith, Shadforth Light Tanker. Two crew, burnover, sheltering in the appliance at the junction of Turner Rd and Shadforth Rd. Over."
Say, in this order: name and rank · appliance · crew · SITREP · location. On your sector or control channel.
AVL: also hold the Distress Button 4 seconds. No reply: Talk-Around, ask for a relay.

Common Radio Issues

And how to fix them: making sure every message is heard.

RADIO USER GUIDE
1WAERN Radios
2Talking on the Radio
3Common Radio Issues

1Hum on the signal

Sounds like a steady low buzz under the voice.

Common cause: a handheld transmitting from its charger, indoors.

If it's you

1
Lift the handheld out of the charger.
2
Step outside, clear of the building.

If you hear it

"Loud, distorted. Hum on your signal, over."

Say "loud and clear" only when it is.

2Two stations at once

Sounds like a warble or squeal. Neither caller is readable.

Happens after "any station?" and on a busy fireground.

If it's you

1
Listen before you transmit.
2
No reply? You weren't heard. Call again.

If you hear it

"Two stations at once. Say again, one at a time, over."

Don't let it pass. Important traffic may be lost.

Report what you hear
Say "clear" only when it is.

3Radio shadow? Use Talk-Around

1
No reply on 173? You may be in a radio shadow. Use Talk-Around only when the IC directs.
2
Switch to 173 Talk-Around and call. Say you're on Talk-Around so they reply on it. Radios on 173 in range hear you direct.
3
Whoever hears you answers on Talk-Around, then relays on 173.
4
Out of the shadow? Cancel Talk-Around: Tait F4 or Vertex A.
