1

Hum on the signal

Sounds like a steady low buzz under the voice.

Common cause: a handheld transmitting from its charger, indoors.

If it’s you
Lift the handheld out of the charger.
Step outside, clear of the building.
If you hear it
“Loud, distorted. Hum on your signal, over.”

Say “loud and clear” only when it is.

2

Two stations at once

Sounds like a warble or squeal. Neither caller is readable.

Happens after “any station?” and on a busy fireground.

If it’s you
Listen before you transmit.
No reply? You weren’t heard. Call again.
If you hear it
“Two stations at once. Say again, one at a time, over.”

Don’t let it pass. Important traffic may be lost.

1

No reply on 173? You may be in a radio shadow. Use Talk-Around only when the IC directs.

2

Switch to 173 Talk-Around and call. Say you’re on Talk-Around so they reply on it. Radios on 173 in range hear you direct.

3

Whoever hears you answers on Talk-Around, then relays on 173.

4

Out of the shadow? Cancel Talk-Around: Tait F4 or Vertex A.
