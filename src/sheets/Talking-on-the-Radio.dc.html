Making a call

1

Listen first. Wait until the channel is clear and you’ve heard OUT.

2

Press PTT, wait one second, then talk.

3

Call them, then say THIS IS and your call sign.

OVERMy turn is finished. A reply is needed.
OUTFinished, no reply. Only one says it.
You“Denmark Chief, this is Shadforth Light Tanker, over.”
Denmark Chief“Shadforth Light Tanker, this is Denmark Chief, go ahead, over.”
You“Arrived on scene, two crew, starting on the north flank, over.”
Denmark Chief“Roger, out.”

Call signsDFES lists when they’re needed: your first call, a station joining or leaving, changing channel, closing down, difficult conditions and multi-agency incidents. Otherwise they can be left out, as in the example.

Moving off the command channel

Keep 173 for command. Crews work on 360, then 361, 362.

1

“CHANGE TO 360, OVER.” Get ready.

2

“WILCO, OVER.” Or say if you can’t.

3

“CHANGE NOW, OUT.” Go to 360.

4

Radio check on 360 straight away.

Emergency message

Extreme emergency only. Everyone else stops transmitting and listens.
“EMERGENCY EMERGENCY EMERGENCY, Denmark Chief, this is Firefighter Smith, Shadforth Light Tanker. Two crew, burnover, sheltering in the appliance at the junction of Turner Rd and Shadforth Rd. Over.”Name and rank, appliance, crew, SITREP, location. On your sector or control channel*.

AVL: also hold the Distress Button 4 seconds. *No reply: Talk-Around, ask for a relay.
