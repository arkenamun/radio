Tait vehicle radio

Every vehicle has the yellow head, which runs a VHF high-band and a UHF radio. Where a yellow handset with a display replaces the black one, it adds a second VHF: three radios in all.

Head: VHF + UHF
Handset: 2nd VHF
Change channel
Key the number on the handset, then Select. Or ▲▼ to scroll, then Done.
Swap VHF / UHF
F3 BAND. The large channel on the display is the radio you talk on.
Hear both radios
F1 DUAL MODE once: both channels show, the one you talk on in large type. A second press transmits on both and shows . Hold F1 for 3 seconds to go back to one radio.
Talk-Around
F4 TALK AROUND: shows top left. F4 again, or change channel, to cancel. Only when the IC directs, or if you declare an emergency and get no reply: then ask to be relayed.
Your location
If asked, describe where you are. Give GPS coordinates only if they’re requested: Menu › [GPS menu item], ▲▼ for latitude/longitude or Zone 50 Easting/Northing.
Scan
F2 SCAN. Keep it off at incidents unless the IC directs.

Vertex VX-829 handheld

Black, VHF high band, yellow aerial.
Key lock
Long press the orange button on top to lock or unlock the keys.
Zone knob
1 DEC/PaW 501–553 · 2 WAERN 101–353 · 3 WAERN simplex 354–369 · 4 WAERN 371–400 · 5 DEC/PaW 601–653.
A Talk-Around
Only when the IC directs, or if you declare an emergency and get no reply: then ask to be relayed.
B Power
Normally left as set.
▲▼ C Channel
Side keys, or C then the three-digit channel (zones 2 to 4). Change zone first, or after an off/on it may go to an unintended channel.
D Scan
Keep it off at incidents unless the IC directs.
Your location
No GPS. Describe where you are; give coordinates only if requested.
Light on top
Red: transmitting · green flash: busy · yellow flash: selcall.

Use it outdoors, away from the vehicle and obstacles. Not indoors, and not for air operations or as ground controller. On 173 it may hear the repeater but not reach it.
